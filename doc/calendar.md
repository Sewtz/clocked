# Task
Instead of forgetting all data when a new day begins, the app shall keep historic data and save them in the persistant storage but in a separate category. So if a new day occurs, instead of deleting the old data, it shall be moved to this category. This shall assure downwards compatibility. In the settings dialog there shall be a button to offer to delete historic data. 

Then a calendar icon shall appear at the top bar next to the gear symbol for options. If clicked, a dialog similar to the options dialog shall appear and display a calendar widget. The calendar shall show a month overview, showing the last 4 weeks from today, the week shall start on mondays and end on sundays. a green circle shall indicate if the day has clock-in or/and clock-out data. the circle shall not differentiate between how many events are logged. If a day is clicked a list with all events shall appear below the calendar widget. The whole dialog shall be scrollable while the app in the background shall not scroll when a dialog is visible.
